Privacy notice

Moona Studio uses optional analytics to understand website use and improve our work. Analytics do not load until you choose “Accept analytics” in the notice on the site. You can choose “Decline” instead.

Analytics services

  • Google Analytics 4, for aggregated traffic and engagement measurement.
  • Microsoft Clarity, for privacy-protected usability insights. The project inquiry form is explicitly masked.
  • PostHog, for product-analytics events such as page views and safe interaction events. Session recording and automatic click capture are disabled for this site.

What we collect

After consent, these services may receive technical and usage data such as page paths, browser and device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and the interaction events needed to understand the site. We do not send values entered in the inquiry form to these analytics services.

Your choices

You can decline analytics in the site notice. If you previously accepted, clear this website’s stored data in your browser and reload the site to choose again. Browser privacy controls such as Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected where available.
