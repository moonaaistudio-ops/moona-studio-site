DUSTLINE · 00:31 · invented brand, product, world and score · one night, zero dialogue · born without a camera

01 The product

Keep it close.

A pressed date‑and‑seed bar that lives in a pocket, built for the long night. The wrapper makes the promise before the film does: a thin cream horizon line, and two words.

02 The idea

The bar is never pitched.

She runs on Dustline and never loses a step. He runs on empty and keeps missing her. It is simply the reason one of them is still standing at dawn.

03 The craft

Nothing in frame stands still.

The same two faces hold through five hours of light on one continuous location. Crowd, dust, wind, fire, all alive. The camera never moves without a reason.

04 The payoff

Planted at dusk. Paid at sunrise.

The same open palm, empty and then filled. In the final frame the horizon printed on the wrapper meets the real one, and the film hands over to the product without a seam.
